          Message

          {first} becomes each candidate's first name and {role} the role title. WhatsApp only allows a business to start a chat with an approved template, so CapLynk sends this text inside your template as one paragraph, up to 1,000 characters.

          Recipients

            Only candidates who agreed to WhatsApp messages can be picked. They agree on Applicant Data, or you can record their consent on their CV. Anyone who replies STOP is skipped from then on.

            Send from Excel

            Download template

            #RecipientNumberMessageSent asResult

            Columns: Recipient Name, Number (with country code) and Custom Message. {first} and {name} in a message become the recipient's first or full name. If the person replied to you on WhatsApp in the last 24 hours, the message goes as you wrote it. Otherwise WhatsApp only lets a business start a chat with an approved template, so CapLynk puts your message inside the caplynk_outreach template as one paragraph, up to 1,000 characters.

            Add role

            Create a role to send bulk WhatsApp messages for. The title and company go into the message, and the role also appears under Roles & matching.

            Add candidates

              Candidates join a role's category on their own when their current title names the role, their skills match it at 60% or more, they're shortlisted on it, or you've messaged them about it. Add anyone else here.
