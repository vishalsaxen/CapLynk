Privacy Policy

CapLynk by Gaplyn Services · Last updated 22 October 2026

CapLynk is a skills-based hiring service run by Gaplyn Services. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use CapLynk, why we use it, who we share it with, and how you can ask us to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account details: name and email address.
  • The CV you upload, and the details we read from it, such as contact information, work history, qualifications and skills.
  • Your WhatsApp number, if you give it, and whether you agreed to receive WhatsApp messages from us.
  • Messages you exchange with us on WhatsApp, and their delivery status.

How we use it

  • To match your skills to roles our clients are hiring for.
  • To contact you about those roles by email, phone or WhatsApp. We only send WhatsApp messages if you have agreed to receive them.
  • To share a summary of your skills profile with a client when you are being considered for one of their roles.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you about your applications.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Our clients, when we put you forward for a role.
  • Google Firebase, which hosts CapLynk and stores your data.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), which delivers our WhatsApp messages.
  • Anthropic, whose AI service helps us read CVs and build skills profiles.

These providers process data on our behalf and only for these purposes.

WhatsApp messages

You can stop WhatsApp messages at any time by replying STOP. We will not message you on WhatsApp again unless you agree to it again.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are an active candidate with us, and delete it when you ask us to or when it is no longer needed for recruitment.

Deleting your data

To have your account, CV and messages deleted, email vaani.s@gaplyn.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "CapLynk data deletion request". We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent at any time by emailing vaani.s@gaplyn.com.
